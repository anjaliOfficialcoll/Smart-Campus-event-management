Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register for an event? ▼
Browse events on the home page, click on any event, then click Register. Fill in your name, student ID, email, and department. After submitting, you'll receive a confirmation email with a QR code.
Where do I find my registered events? ▼
Click My Events in the navigation bar and enter the email address you used when registering. All your registrations will be displayed.
I didn't receive a confirmation email. What should I do? ▼
Check your Spam/Junk folder first. Emails may take 2–3 minutes to arrive. If you still don't receive it, verify the email you entered during registration, or contact the admin.
How do I submit feedback for an event? ▼
Go to the event details page and click Submit Feedback. Enter your registered email, give a star rating (1–5), and write your comments. You must be registered for the event to submit feedback.
Can I register for multiple events? ▼
Yes! You can register for as many upcoming events as you like. Each registration will send a separate confirmation email with a unique QR code entry pass.
What is the QR code used for? ▼
The QR code in your confirmation email is your digital entry pass. Show it at the event entrance for quick check-in. It encodes your registration details so staff can verify your attendance instantly.
